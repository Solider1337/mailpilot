How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription at any time by clicking "My Account" in the add-in and managing your billing via the secure Stripe portal.
Are my emails stored?
No. We process your emails in real-time to generate AI summaries and drafts. The content is securely transmitted, processed in memory, and immediately discarded. It is not used to train AI models.
I paid but the app still says Trial Expired. What should I do?
Please restart your Outlook completely to clear the cache. If the issue persists, contact our support email above.
