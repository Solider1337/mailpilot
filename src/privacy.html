Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how MailPilot AI ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our Outlook Add-in, in compliance with applicable laws including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: We collect your email address via Microsoft Single Sign-On (SSO) to create and manage your account and subscription.
  • Email Content (Transient Processing): To provide AI analysis, the text of the emails you explicitly choose to analyze is securely transmitted to our backend.
  • Payment Information: Handled entirely by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not store your credit card details.

3. How We Process Email Content & AI

Strictly in-transit: The email content you process using MailPilot AI is sent securely to our third-party LLM providers (e.g., OpenAI). This data is processed in-memory and is NOT permanently stored in our databases, nor is it used by us or our AI providers to train foundational AI models.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate MailPilot AI:

  • Microsoft: For SSO authentication and Outlook integration.
  • Stripe: For secure payment processing.
  • Supabase: For secure database hosting (storing your email address and subscription status).
  • Vercel: For secure backend API hosting.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request the deletion of your account and personal data (Right to be Forgotten).
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g., by uninstalling the add-in and cancelling your subscription).

6. Contact Us

If you have any privacy-related questions or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us via our Support page.
