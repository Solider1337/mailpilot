Privacy Policy for MailPilot

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

MailPilot is a client-side application. It processes your emails locally within your browser/Outlook client and sends them directly to the Google Gemini API for analysis. We do not store, log, or transmit your emails to any of our own servers.

2. API Keys

Your Google Gemini API key is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. It is never transmitted to us or any third party, except directly to Google's API servers to perform the requested AI analysis.

3. Third-Party Services

MailPilot uses the Google Gemini API. By using MailPilot, your email content is sent to Google for processing in accordance with Google's API Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

4. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the publisher (Autome).
